Connect your Google Sheet

  1. Open tharihal_plots.csv (already generated for you, with all 271 plots pre-filled) and import it into a new Google Sheet — it already has the right header row: PlotNo, Block, Status, WidthM, LengthM, AreaSqM, AreaSqFt, Facing, PricePerSqYd, Notes
  2. Update Status as plots sell: Available, Booked, Sold, or Hold (plots 272–277 are pre-marked Not for Sale — they're the park/civic-amenity areas)
  3. PlotNo must match the layout's own numbering: plain numbers 1…271 for residential plots
  4. In Google Sheets go to File → Share → Publish to web, choose your sheet and CSV as the format, then click Publish and copy the link
  5. Open this HTML file in a text editor, find sheetCsvUrl near the top of the <script> tag, and paste your link in
  6. Re-upload the file to your host (GitHub Pages works the same way your friend's cricket site does)

The page re-checks the sheet every 30 seconds automatically, plus whenever someone hits Refresh — no backend needed.

⚠ The dimensions in tharihal_plots.csv were OCR'd from your CAD drawing's data table. Most plots follow the standard 9m×15m pattern, and known irregular/corner plots were cross-checked, but please spot-check a few rows (especially plots flagged in the Notes column) against the original drawing before sharing with agents.
